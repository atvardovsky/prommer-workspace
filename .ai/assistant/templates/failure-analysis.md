# Failure Analysis

## Context

Describe the system, goal, and expected behavior.

## Failure And Impact

State what happened, who or what was affected, and the practical consequence.

## Evidence And Timeline

List only evidence-backed events. Mark inferred ordering explicitly.

## Root Cause

Name the first violated contract or incorrect state transition and explain why
it produced the observed failure.

## Contributing Conditions

Separate contributing design, process, observability, validation, and
operational weaknesses from the root cause.

## Recovery

Explain containment and restoration steps.

## Prevention And Validation

Pair each corrective action with a test, check, alert, review gate, or measured
acceptance criterion.

## Remaining Uncertainty

State missing evidence, alternative explanations, and residual risk.
